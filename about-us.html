Our Journey Into the Stars

Discover the story behind Stockholm's premier space museum and planetarium.

Who We Are

Founded in 2015, The Cosmos Museum was born out of a shared passion for astronomy and a desire to make science accessible to everyone. Located in Stockholm, we welcome thousands of stargazers, students, and families every year.

Our mission is simple: to inspire curiosity about the universe and to provide an educational, interactive space where science meets imagination.

The Planetarium Experience

Our state-of-the-art planetarium features an immersive dome screen and advanced digital technology. We bring distant galaxies, exploding stars, and the mysteries of deep space right before your eyes.

Every show is curated by professional astronomers, ensuring an unforgettable journey through time and space for cosmic explorers of all ages.
